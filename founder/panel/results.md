## What would flip a no

- If the free version showed me, without any effort from me, exactly where my salary goes between the 1st and the 20th and that actually helped me save, I'd consider paying for Pro, as long as there were solid reviews or some proof it's safe.
- If it actually cut my Swiggy and Zomato spending in a visible way within the free version first, like showing me I saved a couple of thousand in a month, I might try Pro.
- A friend I trust using it for a few months and showing me it actually filled in their spends on its own, plus real reviews and a clear statement of what data it keeps and who can see it.
- If I used the free version for a month without quitting and it actually stopped my salary vanishing by the 20th, I'd pay the Rs 99.
- If I used the free version for a month without quitting and the auto bank sync actually showed me where my salary goes, then Rs 99 for the AI photo stuff might be worth it.
- Using the free version for a month and finding it got my spends right without me fixing anything, plus seeing real reviews from people like me.
- If it could fill itself from my PhonePe/GPay UPI history without me linking my bank or giving SMS access, plus a few months of real reviews from people like me, I'd try the free version, and maybe Pro after that.
- Using the free version for a month and actually seeing where my salary goes without typing much, plus a few real reviews or a YouTuber I follow vouching for the bank-linking being safe.
- Seeing it still running after a year with real reviews and a clear statement of where my bank data is stored and who can access it.
- A friend or colleague showing me a month where it caught all their UPI spends without duplicates, plus some proof the data is safe, like reviews or a clear privacy policy.
- Real reviews and a clear privacy page showing exactly what data is pulled and stored, plus the app being genuinely useful without the bank link (for example, importing my Splitwise balances) so I can try the free tier for a few weeks first.
- Lots of real reviews from people like me, plus the free version working fully without linking my bank, so I could try it for a couple of months first.
