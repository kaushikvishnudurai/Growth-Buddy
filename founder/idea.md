# Growth Buddy

- What it is: an all-in-one "life OS" app (web PWA + Android/iOS via Capacitor) that runs habits, goals, tasks and reminders, money, health, food and water, notes, a focus timer, a family planner, a peer accountability circle and an AI mentor ("Buddy") in one place, built around asking the user for as little input as possible.
- Who it is for: young professionals, and "lazy" people who want to improve their lifestyle but will not maintain five separate trackers. Founder is in India; the app handles INR, Indian bank feeds (RBI Account Aggregator) and WhatsApp.
- What it sells, at what price: freemium subscription. Free core app; a paid tier is planned but not yet defined or priced (today "premium" is only a visual skin and nothing is charged).
- Where and how: online. Web app hosted on Render (free tier) with TiDB Cloud; Android/iOS wrapper. No acquisition channel decided yet.
- Budget and constraints: solo founder, roughly zero budget (no credit card, free hosting only), building nights and weekends. ~4.5 months of work so far (first commit 2026-06-08, 731 commits, build 117). No user, retention or revenue numbers are known.
