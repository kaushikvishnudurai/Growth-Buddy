Growth Buddy, a phone app (Android, iPhone and web) that keeps your money and daily habits on track with almost no typing.
Link your bank through RBI Account Aggregator and spends fill in on their own; anything it misses, you fix by typing one sentence ("spent 450 on lunch") in the app or on WhatsApp.
Every morning it shows one thing to do today, and a weekly review of where your money and habits went. Habits, water, sleep, food photos and steps are in the same app if you want them.
Free: tracking, quick add, habits and reminders. Pro: Rs 99 a month or Rs 999 a year, adds AI estimates (receipt and food photos), the Buddy AI coach chat, family sharing and deeper reports.
Made by a solo developer in India; new app, no reviews yet.
