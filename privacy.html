Growth Buddy — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

Growth Buddy is a personal wellbeing and habit-tracking app. This policy describes what data the app stores, why, and who else can see it. It is written to describe what the software actually does.

Who is responsible

Growth Buddy is operated by an individual developer. For any privacy question or request, contact growthbuddy.off@gmail.com.

What we collect

CategoryDataWhy
Account Email address, display name, date of birth (optional), avatar image URL, timezone To create and identify your account, and to schedule reminders in your local time
Authentication A hash of your password (never the password itself), session tokens, short-lived email-verification and password-reset codes To keep you signed in securely
Phone number Your WhatsApp number — only if you choose to enable WhatsApp reminders To deliver reminders you asked for. Never used for marketing.
Health & wellbeing Sleep, mood, energy and stress entries; water intake; food entries and photos of meals; habits and streaks; focus-timer sessions; goals, tasks and weekly reviews These are the app's core features — the data you enter is the product
Financial Budgets, transactions and balances you enter in Money Buddy To show your spending. Stored per user, never aggregated or sold.
Household Family member names and dietary preferences, meal plans, pantry and shopping lists Shared meal planning with people you invite
Social Growth Circle posts and challenges, mentorship requests, chat messages with the AI mentor Features you opt into. Circle posts are visible to other members of that circle.
Notifications Browser or device push subscription identifiers To send reminders you enabled

Some of this is sensitive — health information, financial information, photographs of your food, and your date of birth. We are naming it plainly rather than burying it, so you can decide what to put in.

We do not collect location data, contacts, advertising identifiers, or browsing activity outside the app. There is no analytics or advertising SDK.

Who else sees your data

  • OpenAI — the AI features (Buddy chat, quick-add text parsing, food photo estimates, money advice, meal-plan suggestions) send the relevant content to OpenAI's API to generate a response. If you never use those features, nothing is sent.
  • Meta (WhatsApp) — if you enable WhatsApp reminders, your phone number and the reminder text are sent to Meta's WhatsApp Cloud API to deliver the message.
  • Push services — if you enable notifications, reminder text passes through your browser or device vendor's push service (for example Google, Apple or Mozilla).
  • Email provider — verification codes and password resets are sent by email.

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to train models.

How long we keep it

  • Your entries are kept until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Expired sessions, verification codes, password-reset codes and WhatsApp one-time codes are purged automatically every day.
  • Notifications you have read are deleted after 90 days.
  • Internal reminder-delivery records are deleted after 30 days.

Your choices

  • Delete your account — available in the app's settings. This removes your account and the data attached to it.
  • Turn off channels — WhatsApp reminders, push notifications and email digests are each individually optional and can be switched off at any time.
  • Access or correct your data — most of it is editable directly in the app. For anything else, email us.
  • Skip the AI features — the app works without them.

Security

Passwords are stored only as hashes. Session tokens are stored hashed, so a copy of the database alone cannot be used to sign in as you. Traffic is served over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, and we will tell affected users if we become aware of a breach involving their data.

Children

Growth Buddy is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above and notify you in the app.
