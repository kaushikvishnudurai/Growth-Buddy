# scripts/dashboard.js — Home screen

Exports: `ScreenDashboard`, `ScreenFood`, `MiniCalendarCard`, `HOME_WIDGETS`,
`resolveHomeLayout` (check the export block for the exact list).

Every card is a plain function taking a props object and returning DOM. `ScreenDashboard`
assembles them in the user's saved widget order; `resolveHomeLayout(saved)` reconciles the
saved layout against `HOME_WIDGETS` so a new widget appears and a removed one vanishes
without migration. Persisted by `saveHomeLayout` in app.js. Both live in **`scripts/home-order.js`**
(DOM-free, re-exported here) with Home's ordering rules — `sortTasksForHome`, `sortHabitsForHome`,
`habitDue`, `topStreaks`, `homeGoals` — checked by `node scripts/home-order.test.mjs`.
"Today" here is the account's day, not the device's: a module `todayKey()` over `accountZone`,
which app.js `render()` sets on every paint through `setAccountZone(state.user.timezone)`.

**What Home actually renders** (the `renderers` map, one per `HOME_WIDGETS` id): `score` →
`ScoreCard`, `outlook` → `OutlookCard` (outlook-card.js over outlook.js, which is lazy: `loadOutlook()` is awaited with wave 1 in app.js `loadData`, and the widget renders nothing until it has landed: the top insights.js card with its dismiss x and action, then savings / streak pace; dismissals share Report's `uiPrefs.insightsDismissed`; no history fetch on Home), `plan` → `TodayPlanCard`, `wellness` → `WellnessCard`, `tasks` → `TasksCard`,
`calendar` → `MiniCalendarCard`, `habits` → `HabitStrip`, `goals` → `GoalsCard`, `money` →
`MoneyHomeCard` (money-home.js; without a budget the figure reads "spent so far this month" and the week sentence has no eyebrow; carries the repeat-expense "Add it?" nudge, `repeatExpense` in nudges.js, Not today dismisses in memory), `reminders` → `ReminderSuggestionsCard`, `family` → `FamilyMealsCard` (only with `inFamily`; one "Meals today" row → `onOpenFamily`, i.e. app.js `openFamilyMeals`; a family also counts as `started`, and a young account lists it right under the habits, above the checklist); plus `OnboardingCard` on top.
`QuoteCard`, `WeeklyReflectionCard`, `BadgeCard`, `GoalTimelineCard` and `HabitSleepInsightCard`
are defined and exported here but rendered elsewhere (Report, the Habits screen, the quote slot in
app.js); `WaterCard` / `FoodCard` belong to `ScreenFood`.

**First run short-circuits the whole layout:** with no data of any kind yet (tasks, habits,
reminders, money entries, food, water, mood or sleep), `ScreenDashboard` returns the
`OnboardingCard` alone (`.gb-dash--onboarding` centres it, on desktop too). Every widget renders its
own "nothing yet" state, so a new account used to open on nine empty cards saying the same thing
three ways. Adding anything — or dismissing the checklist — brings the full Home back. While
first run's sheet is open (`setupPending`) Home renders empty behind it. **A young account** (data,
checklist still up) gets its own order: habits first, the checklist, the daily brief (`plan`: Home is
Plan my day's only door, so it is never left out), then only widgets that have data (goals, calendar with tasks or reminders, tasks, wellness, score > 0, money); empty ones
stay out until they have something.

**The Quick add bar is always first and is not a widget.** The `quickAdd` prop (app.js's
`homeQuickAdd()`, the same `quickAddBox()` the + sheet uses) is wrapped in a `Card` as
`.gb-dash-qa` and put above everything, the onboarding-only Home included. It stays out of
`HOME_WIDGETS` on purpose, so the layout editor can't hide or reorder it. Null prop = no bar.

| Card | Notes |
|---|---|
| `OnboardingCard` | first-run checklist with a fixed step list for its lifetime: Pick a goal (`onStarterPack`, done with any habit), Tick off your first habit (done on any tick ever, `longestStreak`; the step focuses the first check, `focusFirstHabit`), Get reminded on time (only when `remindersAsk` is set: web push, done only when granted; 'blocked' relabels it "Notifications are blocked" with how to allow it), Add a task for today (`onAddTodayTask`: Due prefilled with today), Log how you feel. Only the steps still left are listed under "N of M done". Dismissal key `gb.onboardDismissed`; the ✕ calls `onOnboardDismiss({ rerender: true })`; the all-done path calls it bare (mid-render) |
| `TodayCard` | the `score` widget, first on Home (above the fold at 390x844): the strip (heading by score, or "All done for today" with the best day streak; the % in mono; one 6px `role=progressbar` bar; mono counts habits owed today, `tasksForToday`, today's reminders done/total from `doneDates`, water %) over **Pending**: overdue tasks, reminders still ahead today, owed habits not ticked, tasks due later today, bills due today/overdue (`billsDue`, Money's upcomingSubs rule narrowed to now; only with the money feature). Each row is a `Check` (list `'pending'`) that completes it (`toggleTask`, `toggleHabit`, `onReminderDone` = app.js `toggleReminderDone` + render, `payBill` = Money's Mark as paid via `onSaveMoney`) and raises an Undo toast. First `PENDING_SHOWN` (5), then "+N more" expands in place (`pendingExpanded`). |
| `DayTimeline` | `habits` AND `tasks` render ONE timeline (the first of the two in the layout draws it with whichever is enabled; the other renders nothing). Head: weekday kicker + date + "N items" over `DayRuler` (day-line.js, shared with calendar.js; a button → `onOpenCalendar`, app.js opens Calendar on today); nothing timed → "Nothing scheduled" + Add (`onAddReminder` = `openReminderSheet`). The routine (`routine` prop = `uiPrefs.routine`, through `validRoutine` / `routineBlocks`, day-line.js) draws as soft bands under the marks with a legend of the kinds present ("Sleep · Lunch · Gym") and its spans in the ruler's aria-label; blocks are not items. No routine → "Add your routine" (`onAddRoutine` = calendar.js `openRoutineDialog(saveRoutine)`; `saveRoutine` re-renders Home). Rows (`tlRow`): mono time, tick (`Check`) or a tag-coloured bell for a reminder, title, sub. Timed = today's reminders (`occursOn`), tasks due today, habits with a `reminderTime`; overdue tasks lead as "Late"; a Now rule sits between past and ahead (account zone, `zoneParts`). Then "Anytime today" (untimed tasks/habits, + Task / + Habit) and a folded `<details>` "Coming up and paused". Task Edit / Start focus / Pause live in `RowMenu`, a native `popover` placed under its "..." button; the title is also an Edit button. Habit rows keep `StreakChain` and `heldHabitOrder`. |
| `GoalsCard` | the `goals` widget: up to 3 open goals, nearest target first (`homeGoals`), each with days left / overdue, the next unticked milestone and the day tracker's count from `goalProgress` (app.js passes `effectiveGoalProgress()`, so a tracker counting from linked habits shows that count). Every row opens Goals (`onOpenGoals`); nothing is edited here |
| `QuoteCard` | quote of the day (cached per day in app.js); not a Home widget |
| `OneThingCard` | pinned above the layout, right under the Quick add bar (not a widget; `HOME_WIDGETS` and saved layouts never see it, and the onboarding-only Home skips it): kicker "One thing today", the title and why from `pickOneThing` (one-thing.js), and one button that calls the existing handler for that kind (`toggleHabit`, `toggleTask`, `onOpenMoney`, `onConfirmSleep`, `onQuickAddWater`, `onAddMood`). A kind with no handler is skipped; null → no card. A sleep guess's title is a `.gb-sleep-guess` button → `onAddSleep` (the sleep dialog, to fix a close-but-wrong night); `WellnessQuick`'s "Slept ...?" text is the same button. The next render re-picks. `oneThingFor` picks it at the top of `ScreenDashboard`, so when the card is asking for sleep or mood, `WellnessQuick`'s row for the same thing is dropped, so is `TodayPlanCard`'s chip for it, and so is the Sleep/Mood tile while it is still unlogged (no nudge twice). While Getting started still lists "Log how you feel", `oneThingFor` passes `skipKinds: ['mood']` (so the checklist is computed first). Water is a candidate only with a non-empty `waterUsualHours` prop (app.js, from /api/water/times) or water logged today. Its block is `.gb-dash-pin`: with `.gb-dash-qa` it spans both desktop columns. |
| `SuggestCard` | `outlook` and `plan` render ONE card (same first-of-pair rule): the Outlook rows (`OutlookCard`'s, dismiss x and action kept), then a foot with `planChips` (what's still open, each its own fix; `skip` as before, plus water while the wellness row is on Home) and Plan my day (`onPlanToday`). |
| `WellnessRow` | the `wellness` widget: `WellnessQuick` (sleep confirm Yes/Edit, the four-mood scale; same one-ask rules) over one inline row of pill buttons: Sleep (hours or Log → `onAddSleep`), Mood (→ `onAddMood`), water % +250 ml (`onQuickAddWater(250)`, only with the water feature). An unlogged chip hides while the one-thing card asks the same kind. |
| `WeeklyReflectionCard` | nudge into the weekly review — rendered on Report, not Home. Week counts: tasks done in the last 7 days (`doneAt`), habit check-ins (`doneThisWeek`, the server's calendar week), goal actions this week from each goal's `recentActions` (the server sends 3 a goal; `actionCount` is all-time, so a goal with all 3 in the week and more on record reads "3+"), sleep/mood days in the last 7; the kcal line only when today has food |
| `BadgeCard` | recent achievements — rendered on Report, not Home |
| `ReminderSuggestionsCard` | suggests reminders from habit/water/wellness gaps; a habit that has its own `reminderTime`, or isn't owed today (`habitDue`), is never suggested. The habit suggestion's time is `suggestReminderTime` (habit-stats.js): the median of the reminder times on the user's other active habits, else 19:00 — the server keeps no time of day for a check-in, so "when they usually tick" isn't knowable. Dropped once that time has passed |
| `GoalTimelineCard` | recent goal actions; `allGoals(sections)` — rendered on Report, not Home |
| `WaterCard` | biggest card (~360 lines). Its head is an `h2.gb-water-title` over `.gb-water-meta` ("N of M ml"). Fish and bubbles are placed by `water-fish.js` (CODEMAP). The stats read drunk / goal / left (`1,250 ml`, `of 2,000 ml`, `750 ml to go`; past the goal "500 ml over goal") beside a graduated scale (`.gb-water-scale`, quarters of the goal); no motivational quotes. Quick-add (always water, `+250 ml` with the unit small); no wave at 0% (`.is-empty`); a pour is module state (`pour`: from-pct, target total, start), so it survives re-renders: the server's reply ~60ms after the optimistic render used to draw the final level with no stream. Every render inside the 1.1s window (`POUR_MS`) draws the stream active with a negative `animation-delay` (picked up, not restarted) and the fill where the rise has got to, then moves it to the current level (the rise starts at `RISE_AT` 180ms, after the stream lands; 520ms transition); a re-render with the same total changes nothing, a new total inside the window only moves the target; no pour under reduced motion (fish and bubbles transition `bottom` on the same curve, holding still in the glass); custom amount with a drink picker (`withDrink`, `DRINKS` from `nutrition.js`; `onQuickAddWater(ml, day, drink)`), goal edit with a one-tap suggestion (`suggestWaterGoalMl(weightKg)`: 33 ml/kg to the nearest 250, shown only with a profile weight and when it differs), entry delete; a non-water row reads "250 ml coffee (counts 200)". `consumedMl` is the server's effective total. The fill is full height and slid down with `translateY` (the summary tumblers use `scaleY`, the pour a scaled fixed height): transforms, not `height`, so no layout per frame. The entry log lists the newest three; past that a "Show all N" button (`showAllWater`, module-level so a delete's repaint keeps it open) unhides the rest in place, so every glass today can be deleted |
| `FoodCard` | today's food summary, grouped by meal slot (`groupBySlot`; a pre-slot row by its logged hour) with each slot's kcal; a row reads "Idli (2 pieces)" when the entry has `pieces` (the server's FoodEntryResponse doesn't return it yet) else "(120 g)"; an empty slot that has begun offers "Copy yesterday's …" (`onCopySlot(slot)`) only when yesterday had that slot (`copyableSlots`, a Set from app.js `yesterdaySlots()`; null = not known yet, no chips); a fibre/sugar/sodium line only for the figures the day has (`dayMicros`); fibre reads "Fibre 12 of 30 g" (`FIBRE_TARGET_G`, FoodWeek's flat target); under the mono kcal total, `.gb-food-measure` splits the day's kcal by slot (aria-hidden: the slot heads carry the numbers) |
| `FoodSummaryLink` / `ScreenSummary` | Food screen's link to `SCREENS.summary`; the screen: nutrient thali (4 katoris on the diagonals, kcal in the centre, % geometry so it shrinks on a narrow phone), calorie split bar, Day by day card (tabs flip panels in place, the pick kept in `summaryNutrient`; chart + top dishes per nutrient), water tumblers (the average uses calories' days, `FoodWeek.averaged`: finished logged days, else today; worded "0.9 L on days you logged" since the bars show all 7), diet check. Everything that looks tappable is: a katori, a calorie share or one of Buddy's pills calls `showNutrient(key, true)` (flips Day by day and scrolls to it); a day's bar calls `onPickDay(date)` (the water tumblers are read-only: a tap ran an AI diet check): Buddy's card reads that day (like Money's day panel: `dayFacts` shows kcal, dishes and water from the loaded week at once, the check fills in under it, and "See these meals in Calendar" is the old `onOpenDay` route). The diet check (Check <day> / Check my week; `onCheck(date?)`, `state.dietCheck.{scope,date}`; a Water pill when the server judged water) and the first load wait with `Thinking()`. Colours come from one `--n` per `.is-<nutrient>` in app.css. Headline and pills read the server's `levels`, except that on a low-calorie week a nutrient is "short" only when its gap is deeper than the calorie shortfall explains, and carbs/fat 15+ points over their calorie-share aim count as "heavy" (calories lead the headline: `levels.calories` is the average against the goal, and the server scales each entry's grams to its logged kcal so the two always agree). Data and invalidation live in app.js `loadWeekSummary` / `invalidateWeek` (fetched on open, not at boot: the food week may run an AI estimate) |
| `MiniCalendarCard` | ~370 lines; the day title is `fmtDate(key, 'weekdayDate')` ("Sun, 11 Oct", the app's style); day badges count open, unpaused tasks due that day (+ one-off reminders); the Food section and total hide when `features.food === false` (the in-place repaint passes no features, so it falls back to the module's `homeFeatures`, set by `ScreenDashboard`); also used on Home, styled by `styles/mini-calendar.css`; app.js repaints it in place via `rerenderHomeMiniCalendarIfActive` |
| `HabitSleepInsightCard` | fitness × sleep correlation over **active** fitness habits (a paused one's frozen streak isn't live); a live streak with nothing else to say reads "6 days of fitness: log sleep each night to see the link"; "Habits today" counts Home's owed habits (`habitDue`), not paused ones. **Returns `null`** without at least one fitness habit *and* one sleep entry — it has no correlation to report, and it used to say so at full card size. Callers must guard on the returned node, not on the function. |
| `ScreenFood` | the standalone Food screen; `.gb-food-screen` puts water and meals side by side at ≥1024px, capped at `min(1100px, 100% - 80px)` for a right gutter; its water and food cards take the measure look (`.gb-food-screen …` rules in app.css: a beaker glass 84 x 160, mono figures). `water-fish.js` sizes the fish to this beaker's 80 x 156 water box: change the beaker, change `GLASS_W/H/R` there |

Styles: `styles/app.css` §"Home: my day, in order" (end of file). The full Home is `.gb-dash--day`
with three zones, each in layout order: `.gb-day-top` (score strip, then Quick add, the break / one-thing
pins, the suggestion), `.gb-day-main` (timeline, wellness row), `.gb-day-rest` (calendar, goals,
reminders, money, family). Below 1024px (phone and tablet) the zones are `display: contents` and blocks take a phone order by `order` (classes `gb-w-<id>`): Today card, pins, the timeline (date hidden, list folded behind "Show the day" / a ruler tap, `dayShown`), Quick add, the suggestion as one row (`.gb-suggest-compact`: insight title toggles the rows, Plan my day), wellness, then the cards; the mini calendar shows the selected week only with a Month toggle (`miniCalMonth`; today's lists hidden in the strip). Pending also lists today's missed reminders ("missed 9:30", after overdue tasks). ≥1024px: a grid, the timeline left
(1.35fr, spanning both rows), top and rest stacked right. Quick add on Home is a slim pill (mic and
language inside, no hint) via `.gb-dash .gb-dash-qa` rules. A young account still uses
`.gb-dash--single` + `balanceHome`.
