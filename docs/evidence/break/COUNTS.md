# I-08 Take a break: inputs before and after

Measured on the flow in `e2e/break.spec.mjs` (a daily habit with a 3-day streak, yesterday missed).

| Keep 3 daily habits alive through a rough patch | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| 3 days | 3 freeze taps per habit per missed day = 9 taps, but the wallet holds 2 tokens (1 a week), so at most 2 days survive; then pause each habit (3 taps each) and mute habit alerts in Settings | 1 tap on Home ("3 days"), or Settings > Alerts > 3 days |
| 7 or 14 days | not possible without pausing every habit and losing the run | 1 tap ("7 days" / "14 days") |
| Freeze tokens spent | 1 per habit per missed day | 0 |
| Habit reminders, evening reflection, digest during the break | still sent unless muted one by one | quiet; timed reminders still ring |
| Score on a break day | drops for every unticked habit | unchanged: nothing is owed |
| Ending early | unpause each habit, unmute alerts | 1 tap ("End now") |
| Coming back | a "0 day" streak | "Back on it. Your streaks are intact." plus one suggested habit to mark done |
